# Current Focus

## Hero Product

`MRnObrainer`

## Current Milestone

Reduce time-to-trust for first-time users.

## Top Release Blockers

1. Onboarding must clearly explain what the app does and what the user should do next.
2. Permission acquisition and recovery must be obvious and resilient.
3. Capture health must answer "is this working?" without requiring technical knowledge.
4. `doctor`, `smoke`, and `ship` must be usable as the default operator path.

## Not Now

- New optional power features that do not shorten bug-to-fix time
- Extra product surfaces that distract from onboarding, trust, or release quality
