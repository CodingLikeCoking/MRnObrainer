# MRnObrainer Product Profile

## Audience

Non-technical users who want a desktop product that reliably remembers what happened on their computer and makes that history useful without requiring setup expertise.

## Promise

MRnObrainer should make the user feel, quickly and clearly, that the app is capturing useful context, staying healthy, and helping them recover information or automate work.

## Primary Use Case

Install the app, grant permissions, confirm capture is healthy, and start using search, rewind, or assistant flows without wondering whether the app is actually working.

## Anti-Goals

- Do not optimize for power-user complexity before the default path is trustworthy.
- Do not hide critical health or permission state behind advanced settings.
- Do not make hosted or enterprise-only flows feel like the core product.
