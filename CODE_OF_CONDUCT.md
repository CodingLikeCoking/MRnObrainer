# Code of Conduct

We want MRnObrainer to be a respectful, technically rigorous project.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, factual, and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but challenge ideas on their merits.
- Respect privacy and security boundaries when sharing repro material.
- Keep discussions focused on the change, bug, design, or evidence at hand.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or intimidation
- Doxxing or sharing private recordings, screenshots, credentials, or internal data
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith review behavior
- Misrepresenting tests, benchmarks, provenance, or authorship

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this
policy and may restrict participation when needed to protect
contributors and the project.

## Reporting

For conduct issues, contact the maintainers privately through the
repository's available maintainer contact path. For security issues,
use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
